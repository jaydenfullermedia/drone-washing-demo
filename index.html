How it works

Tethered drone. Ground crew. Clean building by end of day.

Our drones are tethered to a truck-mounted water and power system, so there's no battery limit and no chemicals stored on your property.

01

Site walk & flight plan

We map your building, check airspace and set up a safety perimeter. Most quotes are turned around in 24 hours from a few photos.

02

Fly & wash

A licensed pilot flies the drone pass by pass while a spotter monitors the perimeter. Pure water or soft-wash solution is fed through the tether at up to 2,000 PSI.

03

Inspect & report

You get 4K before-and-after footage of every elevation, so you can see the results without leaving your desk.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Is drone cleaning actually as good as a window washer on a rope?

For glass and facades, yes — and often better. The drone uses a purified-water system that dries spot-free, and it never skips the awkward corners a person can't reach from a stage. For heavy, caked-on buildup we can run higher pressure than a hand wand ever could.

Is it legal to fly drones this close to buildings in Ontario?

Yes. Our pilots hold Transport Canada advanced RPAS certificates and we file for airspace authorization anywhere it's needed (which, near Pearson or Billy Bishop, is most of the time). We also set up a ground perimeter on every job.

How high can you go?

Our tethered systems comfortably reach 30+ storeys (roughly 100 m). For anything taller, talk to us — it's usually still doable with a rooftop staging setup.

Do you need access to the roof or any anchors?

No. Everything is run from the ground or a parking area. No roof anchor inspections, no swing stage permits, no lift rental.

What about overspray and people on the ground?

We work with soft-wash and pure-water systems that are safe for landscaping and pedestrians, and we cone off the area beneath the flight path. Most jobs happen early morning or weekends when the sidewalk is quiet.

Are you insured?

Fully — $5M commercial general liability, aviation liability for the drones, and WSIB for the crew. Certificates on request with every quote.
