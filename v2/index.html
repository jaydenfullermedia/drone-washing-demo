FAQ

Good questions.

Is it as clean as a window washer on a rope?

Yes — purified water dries spot-free, and the drone doesn't skip corners a stage can't reach. For heavy buildup we run more pressure than a hand wand can.

Can you legally fly this close to buildings?

Our pilots hold Transport Canada advanced RPAS certificates and we arrange airspace authorization wherever it's required, including near Pearson and Billy Bishop.

How tall can you go?

30+ storeys (about 100 m) with our tethered systems. Taller is usually possible with rooftop staging — ask us.

Do you need roof access or anchors?

No. Everything runs from the ground. No anchor inspections, no stage permits, no lift rental.

What about overspray on people or landscaping?

We use pure water and biodegradable soft-wash solutions, cone off the flight path, and schedule early mornings or weekends.

Are you insured?

$5M commercial general liability, aviation liability on the drones, and WSIB for the crew. Certificates included with every quote.
